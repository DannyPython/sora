==============================================================================
Bottom line
==============================================================================
- No break: SORA and SOFR are NOT shown to be cointegrated (Engle-Granger BIC p = 0.183; ECM bootstrap p = 0.624); the anchor test does not reject (bootstrap p = 0.844).
- Allowing one level shift (14 Mar 2025): cointegration holds (Gregory-Hansen -6.43 vs 5% value -4.67). Date is data-chosen: check it against MAS policy dates.
- Sensitive to the start date: EG p (const) from 0.183 to <0.001 across starts; slope 0.60 to 1.54.
- Race: random walk is best at every horizon. Bootstrap Clark-West M1 vs M0 at 1 day: p = 0.028; at 60 days: p = 0.600.
- Headline the data support: no STABLE level anchor over the full window. A SOFR anchor appears only when a regime
  change is allowed (data-chosen date) or the sample starts later, and its slope then varies a lot.
- Open decisions: anchor variable (forward points would make CIP hold by construction), SORA field definition.
